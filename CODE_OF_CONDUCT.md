# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone participating in the LATTRIX community will be treated with respect and dignity.

## Expected Behavior

- **Be respectful**: Value differing opinions and experiences
- **Be inclusive**: Welcome people of all backgrounds
- **Be constructive**: Focus on solutions, not problems
- **Be professional**: Maintain a professional tone
- **Listen actively**: Hear others' perspectives

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Spam or unwanted promotion
- Abuse of power or authority
- Violations of privacy

## Enforcement

Violations will be handled by:
1. Private warning and feedback
2. Temporary suspension from community
3. Permanent removal if necessary

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behavior, contact: conduct@lattrix.dev
